# Copy and feedback

Build a fact sheet from supplied and verified material. Mark prototype fixtures
in delivery notes; never manufacture endorsements, adoption numbers or prices.

- Headings name the thing or task.
- Buttons use a recognizable action, optionally with its object.
- Empty states explain the next useful action.
- Errors describe the problem and recovery without internal IDs or blame.
- Progress describes observed work; do not invent milestones or percentage.
- Success appears only after the action actually succeeds.
- Repeated actions and statuses use consistent wording everywhere.

For a visual reference, keep approved copy and line-break intent. If long real
content strains a layout, repair sizing rather than silently shortening facts.

Feedback should be immediate and stable. Reserve sufficient space for a status
where possible. A toast should not shift the entire conversation. A pane should
open without remounting unrelated content or changing its scroll anchor.
Loading, cancellation, retry and completion must remain distinct states.
